feat(ship): make the commit-record-watch loop one command

pattern-watch flagged "git add -> aitm add -> gh run" at three repetitions
and it was right — that is the sequence this whole session has been typing
out by hand.

Run by hand it drifts, and the step that goes missing is never the commit. It
is the CI watch or the store write: the two that only pay off later, which is
exactly what makes them the ones worth making non-optional.

ship.ps1 runs verify first and refuses to commit on red, reports the branch
rather than assuming it, writes the fact BEFORE waiting on the push so a
dropped connection cannot lose it, and prints the failing log when CI is red
instead of reporting a sha and stopping.
